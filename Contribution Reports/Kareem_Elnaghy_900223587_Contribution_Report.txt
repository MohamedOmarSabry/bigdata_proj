================================================================================
TECHNICAL CHALLENGES AND HOW THEY WERE SOLVED
================================================================================

1. CART ABANDONMENT OVERCOUNTING & WATERMARKING
Challenge: Metrics showed 10x higher abandoned cart values due to duplicate detection. 
Late transactions not matching properly with abandoned cart records.

Solution: Implemented left_anti join deduplication tracking already-reported carts. 
Configured strategic watermarking (15-minute window) for handling late-arriving events.


2. MONOLITHIC STREAM PROCESSING DESIGN
Challenge: Single function managing all 12 streams caused cascading failures, resource 
contention, checkpoint conflicts, and poor maintainability.

Solution: Refactored into 4 independent pipelines each with separate checkpoint directory. 
Enabled isolated failure recovery and individual component restart.
